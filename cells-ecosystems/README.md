## The 26 words

- **Abiotic:** Non-living physical and chemical parts of an environment, such as sunlight, water, rocks, temperature, and soil.
- **ATP:** The primary energy-carrying molecule used by cells to do work (often called the cell's energy "currency").
- **Biotic:** Living or once-living organisms in an ecosystem, including animals, plants, fungi, and bacteria.
- **Cell:** The basic structural and functional unit of all living things; the smallest unit of life.
- **Cell membrane:** A flexible outer barrier that encloses a cell and regulates what enters and leaves.
- **Commensalism:** A symbiotic relationship where one organism benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed.
- **Cytoplasm:** The jelly-like fluid that fills the interior of a cell and holds the organelles.
- **DNA:** The hereditary molecule that carries the genetic instructions for the growth, development, and reproduction of living things.
- **Eukaryotic:** Describes an organism or cell that contains a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles (e.g., plants, animals, fungi).
- **Host:** An organism that provides food, shelter, or resources to another organism, such as a parasite.
- **Mitochondria:** The "powerhouses" of the cell; organelles that convert glucose into cellular energy (ATP) through cellular respiration.
- **Mutualism:** A symbiotic relationship where both species benefit from the interaction.
- **Multicellular:** Made up of more than one cell working together (such as humans, trees, and birds).
- **Nuclear membrane:** The double-layered barrier that surrounds and protects the nucleus, regulating what passes in and out.
- **Nucleus:** The control center of a eukaryotic cell that contains its DNA.
- **Organelle:** A tiny, specialized structure inside a cell that carries out a specific function (like an organ inside a body).
- **Organism:** Any individual living thing, such as a plant, animal, fungus, or bacterium.
- **Parasite:** An organism that lives on or inside a host organism and benefits by causing harm to the host.
- **Parasitism:** A symbiotic relationship where one organism (the parasite) benefits while the other (the host) is harmed.
- **Predator:** An animal that hunts, captures, and feeds on other animals.
- **Prey:** An animal that is hunted and eaten by a predator.
- **Prokaryotic:** Describes a simple, single-celled organism that lacks a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles (such as bacteria).
- **Ribosomes:** Tiny cell structures responsible for assembling proteins.
- **Symbiosis:** A close, long-term interaction between two different biological organisms living together.
- **Unicellular:** Made up of only a single cell (such as an amoeba or bacterium).
- **Vacuole:** A storage sac inside a cell used to store water, nutrients, or waste products.

## Scenario notes

These examples are there so the definitions are used, not just memorized. Each one matches the definition of the correct word.

- A remora rides a shark and eats scraps. The shark is neither helped nor harmed. That is commensalism.
- An orchid sits on a tree and does not take the tree's nutrients. The tree is neither helped nor harmed. That is commensalism. Cattle egrets eating insects stirred up by cattle, while the cattle are neither helped nor harmed, is the same idea.
- A lichen (a fungus and an alga living together), clownfish and a sea anemone, and bacteria in a cow's gut are mutualism: both sides benefit.
- A tick on a dog, a flea on a cat, and mistletoe on an oak are parasitism. The organism is the parasite. The one that is harmed is the host. The relationship is parasitism, not predation. A predator hunts, captures, and eats another animal. A hawk and a mouse, or a wolf and a rabbit, are predator and prey.
- Sunlight, water, rocks, temperature, and soil are abiotic. A living thing, and a dead log that used to be a tree, are biotic.
- Ribosomes assemble proteins. Mitochondria make ATP. The nucleus holds DNA. The nuclear membrane surrounds the nucleus. The cell membrane surrounds the whole cell.
- A bacterium with no nucleus is prokaryotic. An amoeba is one cell with a nucleus, so it is unicellular and eukaryotic. A bird is multicellular. Unicellular and multicellular are about how many cells. Prokaryotic and eukaryotic are about the nucleus.
